## The Reachout → Reform → Resustain Pipeline

Smile Again tracks mothers through a three-stage journey:

1. **Reachout** — Initial contact and enrolment. 200+ per year via outreach, hospital referrals, parish programs, radio.
2. **Reform** — Psychosocial counselling, solidarity group participation, training, and healing. Mothers develop resilience and self-worth.
3. **Resustain** — By two years post-childbirth, mothers are progressing toward self-reliance through livelihood skills, education, or economic opportunity.
