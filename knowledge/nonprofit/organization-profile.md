# Smile Again Families — Organization Profile

## About
Smile Again Families is a nonprofit organisation based in Masaka, Uganda, dedicated to supporting abused, abandoned, and troubled teenage mothers. They provide psycho-social support, emotional and spiritual restoration, healthcare, education, and economic empowerment to young mothers whose lives have been disrupted by teenage pregnancy, sexual abuse, defilement, abandonment, early marriage, poverty, and lack of family support.

**Tagline:** "Healing Hearts, Building Futures, One Family at a time."

---

## Mission
To support abused and abandoned teenage mothers so they can discover their inner worth through emotional, spiritual, and economic restoration.

## Vision
To create strong, sustainable, and reliable families that transform society.

---

## Founded
2014, by Fred and Stella — a Ugandan married couple who began by supporting abandoned teenage mothers in their own community and expanded the mission to address deeper family and childhood brokenness.

## Who They Serve
- Teenage mothers aged 14–22 who dropped out of school due to pregnancy
- Young mothers affected by sexual abuse, defilement, or abandonment
- Mothers and babies in need of health, nutrition, and emotional support
- Girls at risk of early pregnancy (through prevention outreach)
- Families and communities in Masaka District, Uganda

---

## Cumulative Impact (10 Years)
- 1,250 young mothers reached
- 798 lives transformed
- 50 community solidarity groups formed
- 107 mothers supported in vocational skills training
- 6 mothers supported back to school
- 8 Catholic parishes engaged

---

## Operating Model
Smile Again works through a three-stage model:

**1. Reachout** — More than 200 mothers reached each year through one-on-one community outreach, home visits, hospital referrals, parish programs, radio announcements, and community engagement.

**2. Reform** — Psychosocial counseling, solidarity group formation, and training that promotes healing, resilience, and self-reliance.

**3. Resustain** — By two years after childbirth, mothers are supported toward self-reliance through livelihood opportunities and long-term support systems.

---

## Strategic Objectives
- Sensitize families and communities about teenage pregnancy
- Enrol teenage mothers into holistic programs
- Restore hope and inner worth
- Strengthen family and community support systems
- Empower mothers and families with livelihood skills
