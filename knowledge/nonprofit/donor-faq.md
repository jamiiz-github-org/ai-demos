# Smile Again Families — Donor & Partner FAQ

## About the Organisation

**What is Smile Again Families?**
Smile Again Families is a nonprofit organisation based in Masaka, Uganda. Founded in 2014 by Fred and Stella, it supports abused, abandoned, and troubled teenage mothers through psychosocial support, education, healthcare, and economic empowerment. Their tagline is "Healing Hearts, Building Futures, One Family at a time."

**Where do they operate?**
Primarily in Masaka District, Uganda. Operations are based at the Guadalupe Mission Home on Masaka-Kyotera Road. Their 50 solidarity groups and community outreach extend across multiple neighbourhoods and parishes in the Masaka region.

**Are they a registered organisation?**
Yes. They are registered in Uganda, and have a US-based 501(c)(3) partner — Smile Again Families Foundation — which enables tax-deductible giving from US donors.

**How long have they been operating?**
Since 2014 — over 10 years of work in Masaka.

---

## Impact

**How many people have they helped?**
Over 10 years: 1,250 mothers reached, 798 lives transformed, 50 community solidarity groups formed, 107 mothers completed vocational training, 6 mothers supported back to formal school, 8 Catholic parishes engaged.

**How many mothers do they reach each year?**
More than 200 mothers per year through outreach, hospital referrals, parish programs, radio announcements, and direct community engagement.

## Donating

**How can I donate?**
Via the Support page at smileagainfamilies.org. General donations go directly to supporting young mothers and children through food, childcare, education materials, counselling, and staff support.

**What are the suggested giving levels?**
- $45/month — supports a girl's return to school
- $50/month — provides essential care and counselling
- $100/month — supports livelihood and skills training
- $500/year — helps sustain a beneficiary in the Safe Home
- $1,000+ — transforms multiple lives across programs

**Can I sponsor a specific girl's education?**
Yes. The Smiling Back to School project allows donors to sponsor individual young mothers returning to secondary school or vocational/nursing training. Several mothers are currently listed for sponsorship on the website. Costs range from $150/term for secondary school to $550/semester for nursing programs, plus approximately $50/term or semester for school requirements.

**What is the Safe Home and how can I support it?**
The Safe Home (Guadalupe Mission Home) provides shelter for abandoned teenage mothers. Construction is complete. They are now fundraising for operating costs — food, medical care, hygiene materials, school supplies, and childcare. Donate and mark your gift "Safe Home Operations."

**Is my donation tax-deductible?**
For US donors giving through Smile Again Families Foundation (the 501(c)(3) partner), yes. Check the website for current giving instructions.

## Volunteering

**Can I volunteer?**
Yes. Smile Again welcomes volunteers at the SAFF Center and in field activities. Volunteers can support care, skills training, childcare, workshops, counselling sessions, mentorship, events, fundraising, awareness campaigns, and safe space maintenance.

**What qualifications do I need?**
Passion for helping teenage mothers and vulnerable children is the most important thing. Communication, teamwork, confidentiality, patience, and empathy are key. Experience in childcare, education, counselling, or community service is helpful but not required.

**How long do I need to commit?**
Minimum one week. A certificate is issued upon completion.

## Partnerships

**Do you partner with faith-based organisations?**
Yes. 8 Catholic parishes are active partners. FOCUS (Fellowship of Catholic University Students) sends missionaries to Uganda annually to support Smile Again's work with mothers and parish communities.

**How can my organisation partner with Smile Again?**
Contact them directly at smileagain.families@gmail.com or via the contact form at smileagainfamilies.org. They welcome partnerships with funders, faith communities, health organisations, schools, and international volunteers.
