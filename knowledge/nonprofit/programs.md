# Smile Again Families — Programs & Projects

Smile Again Families runs six core programs serving teenage mothers and their children in Masaka, Uganda.

---

## 1. Safe Home / Guadalupe Mission Home

The Safe Home provides shelter for abandoned teenage mothers who have been rejected by their partners or family members. Abandonment can lead to despair, risk of unsafe work, abortion risk, or even suicidal thinking — the Safe Home provides a stable, supportive environment to prevent these outcomes.

**What the Safe Home provides:**
- Daily support, shelter, and safety
- Antenatal and postnatal care
- Training and workshops
- Space for children's care (children's centre with play and daycare space)
- A future kindergarten is planned

The Guadalupe Mission Home also serves as a training and workshop centre for teenage mothers who are not fully accommodated on-site.

**Current status:** Construction of the Safe Home is complete. Fundraising is ongoing for operating costs — food, medical care, school supplies, hygiene materials, and childcare support. Donations for this should be marked "Safe Home Operations."

---

## 2. Smiling to Improve Livelihood

This program supports teenage mothers and their babies with basic needs: food, clothing, antenatal supplies, baby clothes, diapers, cotton, sanitary towels, and daily essentials. Many mothers cannot afford two meals a day, clothing for their babies, antenatal requirements, clean water, or medical bills.

**Goals include:**
- Increase clean-water access in at least 3 communities per year
- Provide solar lighting in at least 3 communities per year
- Support at least 5 young mothers in accessing decent housing annually

---

## 3. Smiling Body (Health Program)

This program focuses on improving the physical health of teenage mothers and their babies.

**Activities include:**
- Health education
- Malaria and HIV/AIDS prevention
- Nutrition programs
- Medical support
- Partnerships with healthcare units and individuals
- Food supplements, medical supplies, and health camps

---

## 4. Solidarity Groups

Smile Again forms solidarity groups based on where teenage mothers live. These groups provide social and emotional support, a sense of belonging, peer encouragement, and ongoing monitoring.

**How it works:**
- Groups are community-based, formed by location/neighbourhood
- Smile Again staff visit groups bi-weekly for group and individual psychosocial support
- 50 solidarity groups currently active across Masaka and surrounding communities
- Leaders are identified within groups and mentored for ongoing community impact

---

## 5. Stay Smiling Project (Prevention)

This project focuses on preventing teenage pregnancy through education, community engagement, and research.

**Activities include:**
- Smile Again clubs formed in schools
- Sensitisation sessions on the dangers of teenage pregnancy
- Community awareness campaigns
- Village dialogues with local leaders and policymakers
- Research on teenage pregnancy and teen well-being to support informed decisions

---

## 6. Smiling Back to School

This project helps teenage mothers return to formal education or vocational training so they can rebuild their futures and support their children. Donors can sponsor specific young mothers.

**Sponsorship costs:**
- Secondary school tuition: approximately $450/year, plus $50/term for school requirements
- Nursing or vocational training: approximately $550/semester plus $50/semester for requirements

## FOCUS Mission Partnership

FOCUS (Fellowship of Catholic University Students) missionaries travel to Uganda each year to support Smile Again's work with teenage mothers, babies, and parish communities — both onsite and in the field.
