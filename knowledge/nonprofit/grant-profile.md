## Grant Messaging

**Problem statement:** Uganda has one of the highest teenage pregnancy rates in sub-Saharan Africa. In Masaka District, teenage mothers face abandonment, poverty, social stigma, and school dropout — creating a cycle of inter-generational vulnerability. Many lack access to shelter, antenatal care, psychosocial support, or economic opportunity.

**Theory of change:** When a teenage mother receives emotional restoration, stable shelter, health support, and an economic pathway, she can break the cycle of poverty — for herself and for her child. Smile Again's Reachout → Reform → Resustain model ensures no mother is left at the point of crisis.

**Evidence of impact:** In 10 years, 798 lives transformed, 1,250 reached, 50 solidarity groups active, 107 vocational completions, Safe Home built, 8 parishes engaged.

**Sustainability:** Community-based solidarity groups are self-sustaining after formation. Graduate mothers become peer mentors. US 501(c)(3) structure enables diversified international funding. Parish networks reduce dependency on any single donor.
