# Asante Stays — Local Guide to Riverside, CA

Welcome to Riverside! Here are our personal recommendations for making the most of your stay.

---

## Getting Around
- **Car:** Highly recommended — Riverside is a driving city. Parking is generally easy.
- **Rideshare:** Uber and Lyft are widely available
- **Metrolink:** Riverside Downtown Station connects to Los Angeles (about 75 mins)
- **Airport:** Ontario International Airport (ONT) — 30 mins. LAX — 60–75 mins depending on traffic.

## Things to Do

### Historic & Cultural
- **Mission Inn Hotel & Spa** — a National Historic Landmark, stunning Spanish Revival architecture. Worth visiting even just to walk through the lobby. Has a great restaurant and spa.
- **Riverside Art Museum** — local and visiting exhibitions, very accessible
- **California Museum of Photography** — on the UCR campus, free and excellent
- **Fox Performing Arts Center** — check their events calendar for concerts and shows

### Outdoor & Nature
- **Mount Rubidoux Park** — 5 mins away. An iconic Riverside hike with panoramic city views. Sunrise is especially beautiful. Easy to moderate trail.
- **Fairmount Park** — large park with a lake, walking paths, and picnic areas, great for families
- **Box Springs Mountain Reserve** — more challenging trails with great views
- **Lake Perris State Recreation Area** — 20 mins. Swimming, boating, fishing, camping.

### Day Trips from Riverside
- **Joshua Tree National Park** — 1 hr 15 mins. One of Southern California's most stunning parks. Worth at least a full day.
- **Palm Springs** — 45 mins. Great for shopping, architecture, and the aerial tramway
- **Big Bear Lake** — 1 hr 30 mins. Skiing in winter, hiking and kayaking in summer
- **Los Angeles** — 60 mins (off-peak). Hollywood, beaches, museums, world-class dining
- **San Diego** — 1 hr 30 mins. Beaches, Balboa Park, world-famous zoo
- **Disneyland** — 45 mins from Riverside

### Shopping
- **Galleria at Tyler** — 10 mins, major mall with all main retailers
- **Riverside Food Lab** — food hall with local vendors, great for lunch or dinner
- **Downtown Riverside** — walkable shopping and dining district along Main Street

## Local Tips
- Riverside gets hot in summer — June–September temperatures regularly exceed 95°F (35°C). The AC at the property is your friend.
- The Mission Inn's Festival of Lights in December is one of the largest outdoor light displays in the US — genuinely spectacular if you're visiting in winter.
- Parking downtown is generally easy and often free. Most meters are generous.
- Rideshare surge pricing kicks in Friday and Saturday evenings near the bars on University Ave.
