# Asante Stays — Property Overview

## Welcome to Asante Stays
Asante Stays is a beautifully maintained property in Riverside, California offering three distinct listings — a cozy studio, a spacious two-bedroom apartment, and a large shared house — all on the same stunning compound at 4682 Bandini Avenue.

"Asante" means "thank you" in Swahili, and that warmth is woven into every detail of every stay.

The compound is a standout feature shared by all three listings: lush green grounds filled with fruit trees, creating a serene, private atmosphere that guests consistently love.
