## Which Listing Is Right for You?

### Choose the Studio if:
- You're travelling solo or as a couple
- You want a quiet, compact private space
- You're on a shorter stay or tighter budget
- You just need a comfortable base to sleep and work

### Choose the Two-Bedroom if:
- You're a small family or two guests travelling together
- You want two proper separate bedrooms
- You want a full kitchen and a dining table
- You need more space than a studio but a private self-contained unit

### Choose the Shared House if:
- You're a group or family that wants to be together
- You need a proper home office or workspace
- You're doing a longer stay and need laundry facilities
- You want the most space and the full home experience
- You love natural light — the sunroof is a genuine highlight
- You want to cook and dine together in a wide, open space
