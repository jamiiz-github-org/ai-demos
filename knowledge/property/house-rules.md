# Asante Stays — House Rules

These rules apply to all three listings at 4682 Bandini Avenue: the Studio (Unit 4664), the Two-Bedroom (Unit 4664B), and the Shared House.

We want you to feel at home. These rules help us keep the property in great shape for every guest.

## Guests
- Maximum 6 guests unless additional guests were approved at booking
- Please let the host know if anyone beyond the booked number will be visiting
- No unregistered overnight guests

## Noise & Neighbours
- Quiet hours: 10:00 PM – 8:00 AM
- Please be mindful of neighbours — this is a residential neighbourhood
- No outdoor music after 9:00 PM

## Smoking
- No smoking anywhere inside the property
- Smoking is permitted in the backyard only — please use the ashtray provided and dispose of waste properly
- Any smoke smell inside the property will result in a cleaning fee

## Pets
- Pets are welcome with prior approval — please message the host before booking with a pet
- A pet fee applies per stay
- Please do not allow pets on the furniture or in the bedrooms
- Clean up after your pet in the yard and surrounding areas

## Parties & Events
- No parties, events, or large gatherings without prior written approval from the host
- Unauthorised events will result in immediate cancellation of the reservation

## Parking
- 2 parking spaces in the driveway — included
- Please do not park on the lawn
- Street parking is available on weekdays

## Kitchen & Cleanliness
- Please clean up after cooking — leave the kitchen as you found it
- Do not leave food out — it attracts pests in the California heat

## Trash
- Bin bags are under the kitchen sink
- Black bin = general waste, Blue bin = recycling, Green bin = garden waste
- Leave rubbish bags in the garage bin before checkout

## Outdoor Space
- The backyard is yours to enjoy — please leave it tidy
- The outdoor furniture should be returned to its original position before checkout
- No open fires or barbecue grills unless the provided BBQ is used

## Damage & Liability
- Please report any damage or breakages immediately via the booking platform
- Guests are responsible for damage caused during their stay
- Emergency contact information is in the welcome guide on the kitchen counter

## Check-In / Checkout
- Check-in: 3:00 PM | Checkout: 11:00 AM
- Self check-in — entry code sent 2 hours before arrival
- Studio (Unit 4664): door is right next to the garage
- Two-Bedroom (Unit 4664B): entrance is on the right side of the building
- Shared House: main front door
- Late checkout must be requested in advance and is subject to availability
