# Jamiiz AI — Frequently Asked Questions

## About Jamiiz AI

**What does Jamiiz AI do?**
We build practical AI systems for service businesses — AI agents, document assistants, and workflow automations that eliminate repetitive admin, speed up customer responses, and free your team for higher-value work.

**Is this just a chatbot?**
No. Generic chatbots answer based on general training data. Jamiiz AI systems are trained on your specific business content — your policies, your workflows, your documents, your voice. The result is a system that knows your business, not just AI.

**What types of AI systems do you build?**
Three main types: Customer Support Agents (handle enquiries, triage, escalation), Document Assistants (search policies, SOPs, contracts, internal knowledge), and Admin Workflow Automations (convert emails, forms, and requests into tasks and follow-ups automatically).

**Are you a software product or a services company?**
We are a services company. We build custom AI systems specifically for your business — we don't sell a generic tool you configure yourself.

## Technical

**What AI models do you use?**
We select models based on the task — typically OpenAI GPT-4o-mini for fast Q&A and Anthropic Claude for complex document reasoning. We choose the most cost-effective model that meets your requirements.

**Where is our data stored?**
Your documents and business content are stored securely using enterprise-grade vector databases. Your data is never shared with other clients or used to train AI models.

**What documents can the AI learn from?**
PDF, Word documents (.docx), plain text, and markdown files. Any document your team currently reads to find answers.

**Can it integrate with our existing tools?**
Yes. We can connect AI workflows to email, CRM systems, Slack, Google Workspace, forms, and more. The integration plan is included in the Sprint.

**What security measures are in place?**
Every system includes human escalation paths, guardrails for unsafe or out-of-scope queries, and audit-friendly workflow logging. We do not ship systems without a clear escalation path.
